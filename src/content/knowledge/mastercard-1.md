# Mastercard — Rapid Iterations (2021, ~2 months)

**Client:** Mastercard (via Designit, a global design consultancy)
**Role:** Senior Design Technologist
**One-liner:** Rescued a stalled, frustrating engagement by co-designing live in Figma — turning stakeholder conversations directly into sketches, trust, and approved screens.

## The situation
This was less a UX-craft project and more about **approach and meeting the client
where they were.** The client team had a vision for a streamlined internal
process for data ingest and visualization, but the existing team was struggling
to translate their requirements and desires into designs that matched what they
wanted. The client was frustrated, and Phong was brought in to lead and bring a
fresh perspective.

## What Phong did
Rather than restart with more workshops and re-interviewing every stakeholder, he
**dove straight in**:

- On virtual calls, he opened **Figma live** and had stakeholders describe the
  project and its features while he **sketched the systems and flows in real
  time.** His technical background — understanding database systems and ETL
  flows — let him keep up and interpret their vision accurately.
- Each session produced sketches and workflows the client felt good about, which
  **built the trust** to keep the conversation going.
- As his confidence in interpreting their vision grew, he worked with the
  designers to flesh out screens, showed them to the client, got approval, then
  moved to the next set to sketch live. Each day was driven toward that day's
  priority — wireframes, user stories, or screens to react to.
- Because the team lived in the Figma files daily, the **prototypes doubled as
  slide presentations**, with each workflow linked dynamically as it matured.

## Outcome / impact
Unblocked a frustrated client by converting ambiguous requirements into concrete,
approved wireframes and workflows through live co-design — restoring momentum and
trust in a short, high-pressure engagement.

## Skills demonstrated
Live co-design/facilitation, technical fluency (databases, ETL), rebuilding
client trust, rapid iteration under pressure, consulting delivery for large
enterprise/financial clients.
