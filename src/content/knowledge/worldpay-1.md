# Worldpay — Workshops for Asset Consolidation (2021, ~3 months)

**Client:** Worldpay / FIS (via Designit)
**Role:** Senior Design Technologist
**Subtitle:** Design Thinking to plan for streamlining.
**One-liner:** On a three-person team, used fully-virtual Design Thinking workshops to plan the consolidation of fragmented portals into one unified platform — then coded the Angular front-end components as a bonus deliverable.

## The situation
After a series of acquisitions and mergers, Worldpay was left with a **multitude
of technologies, each with its own developer tools and portals.** Designit was
brought in as a consulting team to assess, recommend, and formulate a solution to
**merge the portal experiences into a unified platform.** The team was three
people with three months to execute.

## What Phong did
- **Research, workshops, and synthesis.** Phong contributed research,
  co-facilitated workshops, and shared the architecture and UX work; a visual
  specialist extended the design system.
- **Fully virtual facilitation.** Travel wasn't possible at the time, so
  everything — including client workshops — ran virtually. They reviewed
  workflows and architecture and sketched ideas over screen sharing, and ran
  individual brainstorming and worksheets in a **Miro** whiteboard. The three
  consultants **took turns facilitating, cleaning up the boards, and taking
  notes** to move stakeholders from activity to activity.
- **Coding as an add-on deliverable.** Unplanned: as delivery neared, the client
  was rushed to get implementation running. Phong asked about their tech stack
  and — since he could code **AngularJS** — offered to build the design system
  and UI components as an add-on. When they agreed, he spent a **two-week sprint
  coding their front end.**

## Outcome / impact
Delivered a consolidation plan and unified-platform direction from fragmented,
post-merger portals — plus a working set of Angular components that let the
client's own developers move straight into implementation.

## Skills demonstrated
Design Thinking facilitation, remote/virtual workshop craft, systems architecture
and consolidation strategy, front-end coding (AngularJS), consulting delivery.
