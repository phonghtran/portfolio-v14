# Obviously — First Design System / Design Foundation (2020–2021, ~1 year)

**Client:** Obviously (a ~9-year influencer-marketing startup with a custom SaaS platform; acquired by WPP/VML in 2022)
**Role:** Director of Design (was Lead Designer) — Obviously's **first UX designer hire**
**One-liner:** Built Obviously's entire design practice from scratch — a new design system, a research function, and a workshop culture — after years of engineer-led design.

## The situation
Obviously runs influencer campaigns end-to-end: finding influencers, managing
content creation, and collecting performance analytics — all on a custom SaaS
platform. Before Phong, design was handled by the Engineering team and occasional
freelancers. Years of engineer-led decisions had produced **hundreds of
inconsistencies at the component, page, and architecture levels**, made worse by
half-hearted CSS do-overs that made fixes non-trivial.

## What Phong did
He ran a phased transformation rather than a risky big-bang rebuild:

- **Complete UI audit** of every page to catalog the inconsistencies.
- **Interviewed the Director of Engineering** to understand the history of the
  UI and CSS front-end stack.
- **Built a new design system** — typography, colors, layouts, and components —
  on top of the existing Bootstrap framework.
- **Partnered with Product** to find where the system could be applied safely,
  overhauled an isolated part of the app to re-do the CSS architecture under the
  new system, then **progressively enhanced sub-system by sub-system** until the
  transformation was complete.

He also rebuilt the company's **design research** from the ground up — dozens of
interviews with internal users, influencers, and clients — and instituted user
personas, buyer personas, prototype-based testing, design sprints, and journey
mapping.

As the solo designer he **facilitated 20+ workshops** (Miro for remote-first,
post-its and sharpies in person) — not just for design, but for executive
decision-making, internal process, and departmental restructuring. Outputs
ranged from quarterly roadmaps and new service workflows to new products for the
sales team, and sometimes simply alignment on a business problem.

## Later work in this role
- **Efficiency via AI & automation:** audited the full execution process
  (pre-sales → campaign reporting), assessed a dozen-plus tools and vendors, and
  iterated the platform toward better notifications and automated reporting to
  cut team micro-management.
- **Design Ops:** reworked the design→engineering handoff (Looms + sample
  front-end code), inserted a **Design QA step** into every design JIRA ticket
  (filed as non-blocking low/high-priority tickets), and built a JIRA-based
  design-request flow as internal visual-design demand grew.
- **Marketing:** updated buyer personas and helped organize a product-specific
  marketing plan as an internal marketing team ramped up.
- **Acquisition transition:** when Obviously was acquired by a global ad
  conglomerate, rapidly expanded the visual language for the new partnership and
  parent-company client relationships.

## Outcome / impact
Established Obviously's design practice as its first design hire: first design
system, first real research function, and a workshop-driven decision culture —
the foundation the later platform work (see the Tech Debt and AI projects) was
built on.

## Skills demonstrated
0→1 design-practice building, design systems, CSS/front-end architecture,
progressive rollout strategy, user research, workshop facilitation, Design Ops,
